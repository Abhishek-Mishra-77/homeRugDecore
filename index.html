Bhadohi: Where Every Thread Tells an Artisan Story.

For over four centuries, the town of Bhadohi in Uttar Pradesh has preserved India’s most revered weaving traditions. Every single HomeRugDecore rug is tufted by hand with New Zealand wool, washed in clean mineral water, hand-sheared for velvety softness, and finished with organic backing.

By partnering directly with artisan families, we bypass middlemen to ensure ethical wages for master tufters and direct-to-consumer pricing for homeowners across 30+ countries worldwide.
